Congratulations on being selected for the 2026 District Clinic Honor Band and Choirs!

Reminders

All students

  • Practice and prepare music before the clinic
  • Write measure numbers on all music
  • Bring concert attire for Tuesday's performance

Essentials

  • Band: instrument, music, pencil, water bottle, and any mutes your part calls for
  • Choirs: music, pencil, water bottle, black folder

Times to avoid parking and hallways

Classes are in session both days. Hallways are busy during South's passing periods:

  • 7:30–7:45
  • 9:10–9:35
  • 11:00–11:50
  • 1:15–1:20
  • 2:45–3:15

Parking

  • Bus drop-off: Main Entrance, during non-peak times
  • Bus parking: Northeast Parking Lot
  • Student parking: Student lot (non-painted spaces), north of school

For directors

  • Hospitality room: Drama Room (D114), both days
  • Directors meeting: Tuesday at 8:30, Drama Room (D114)
